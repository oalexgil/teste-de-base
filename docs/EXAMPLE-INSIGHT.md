# Example Insight

A useful AdSignal result should sound like this:

> Campaign A has the lowest CPL, but only a small share of its leads qualifies. Campaign B costs more per raw lead yet produces a lower CPQL and CAC. Do not scale Campaign A based on CPL alone.

A useful creative result should sound like this:

> The “Volume” angle attracts cheaper clicks but produces weak downstream lead quality. The “Control” angle has a higher media cost but a stronger qualified-to-sale outcome. Test new hooks around the winning angle instead of producing more cosmetic variants of the cheap-click concept.

These examples illustrate the core product philosophy: acquisition economics and creative decisions must be connected to downstream quality.
