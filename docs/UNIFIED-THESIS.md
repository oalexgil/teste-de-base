# Unified Thesis

AdSignal combines three pains because they are causally connected:

- media efficiency determines the cost of acquiring attention and conversions;
- lead quality determines whether those conversions have commercial value;
- creative strategy influences both who clicks and the quality of the demand created.

Optimizing any one layer in isolation can produce a misleading local optimum. The product should therefore evaluate the acquisition system as one chain from spend to customer outcome.
