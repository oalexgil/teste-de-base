# Product Summary

**AdSignal Intelligence** is a unified paid-media decision layer for teams that need to understand not just how many leads ads generate, but whether acquisition is efficient, leads are commercially useful, and creative concepts are worth further investment.

The product combines:

- media economics and funnel leakage;
- downstream lead quality and CAC;
- creative concept health and diversity;
- prioritized, evidence-backed actions.

The V0.1 branch is a functional analytical foundation plus a demonstrable dashboard. It intentionally defers expensive integrations until the core recurring value is validated.
