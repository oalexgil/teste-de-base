# AdSignal Go-to-Market Hypothesis

## Beachhead

Start with operators who already feel the pain of reconciling ad-platform metrics with lead quality:

1. freelance performance marketers;
2. boutique lead-generation agencies;
3. service businesses with a CRM and meaningful monthly ad spend.

## Message

Avoid positioning as “another dashboard” or “AI for ads”. Lead with the financial decision:

> Your cheapest leads may be your most expensive customers. AdSignal connects ad spend, qualified leads and creative performance so you know what to fix next.

## Self-serve acquisition

- interactive demo with realistic data;
- free CSV report as the primary acquisition hook;
- comparison content: CPL vs CPQL, cheap leads vs profitable leads;
- SEO pages around paid-media diagnosis and creative testing;
- shareable executive report that naturally exposes the product to agency clients.

## Product-led growth loop

1. Operator imports data.
2. AdSignal finds a high-impact issue.
3. Operator exports/shares report.
4. Client or teammate sees branded report.
5. New workspace/user enters the product.

## Avoid initially

- expensive sales organization;
- promises of guaranteed ROAS improvement;
- opaque “AI optimization” claims;
- auto-changing ad accounts before trust is established;
- building every connector before CSV retention is proven.
