# Vision

AdSignal should become an operating layer between advertising platforms and business outcomes.

The long-term product should understand four connected layers:

1. **Delivery** — what the ad platforms spent and delivered.
2. **Behavior** — what happened after the click.
3. **Quality** — which leads became qualified opportunities and customers.
4. **Creative cause** — which messages, angles and formats are associated with those outcomes.

The product wins if it reduces the time between “performance changed” and “the team knows the most defensible next action.”
