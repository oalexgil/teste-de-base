# AdSignal Metrics Reference

## Paid media
- CPM = spend / impressions × 1,000
- CTR = clicks / impressions
- CPC = spend / clicks
- landing-page view rate = landing-page views / clicks
- CVR = leads / landing-page views (fallback: clicks)
- CPL = spend / leads
- ROAS = tracked revenue / spend

## Lead economics
- qualification rate = qualified leads / leads
- meeting rate = meetings / leads
- lead-to-sale rate = sales / leads
- qualified-to-sale rate = sales / qualified leads
- CPQL = spend / qualified leads
- cost per meeting = spend / meetings
- CAC = spend / sales
- revenue per lead = revenue / leads

## Creative intelligence
Creative performance is grouped by a concept key composed from:

`hook × angle × format`

This prevents a large number of cosmetic ad variants from being mistaken for genuine strategic diversity.

## Scores
Scores are directional product diagnostics, not universal industry benchmarks. They must be calibrated with real customer cohorts before marketing claims are made about predictive accuracy.

Every score must remain decomposable into observable metrics and must never be presented as a probability of future success.
