# AdSignal Intelligence — Brand Direction

## Positioning line
**Paid-media intelligence that connects spend, lead quality and creative performance.**

## Primary promise
Know what changed, why, and what to do next.

## Tone
- analytical, not hype-driven;
- concise and financially literate;
- evidence before recommendation;
- confident without implying certainty where attribution is incomplete.

## Visual direction
The V0.1 dashboard uses a dark analytical interface with a restrained green signal color. The product should feel closer to an operating console than a generic marketing dashboard.

## Terminology
Prefer:
- diagnosis
- evidence
- signal
- acquisition economics
- qualified outcome
- creative concept
- confidence

Avoid:
- guaranteed profit
- magic AI
- winning formula
- perfect attribution
- automatic scaling claims
