# AdSignal — Commercial Plan (September 2026)

## Positioning

AdSignal is a **Paid Media Decision Engine**, not another reporting dashboard.

Core promise:

> Connect ad spend, lead quality, creative performance and prior decisions to show what changed, how much budget is exposed, and what action should be tested next.

Primary wedge:

> Your cheapest lead can be your most expensive customer.

## Beachhead ICP

Prioritize buyers with enough spend and downstream data for the problem to be painful:

1. boutique performance agencies managing multiple lead-generation clients;
2. freelance media buyers managing 5+ accounts;
3. B2B / service startups with paid acquisition + CRM;
4. high-value lead businesses: clinics, education, real estate, solar, insurance and professional services.

Deprioritize advertisers with tiny budgets, no CRM outcomes, or too little conversion volume to learn from.

## Phase 1 — Evidence before fundraising

Goal: prove recurring use and willingness to pay.

Target proof points:
- 10 design-partner workspaces;
- at least 3 paying customers;
- at least 2 monthly re-import cycles per active customer;
- recommendations that users mark as useful / acted on;
- at least one case where CPL and downstream CAC point to different budget decisions;
- paid-media spend analyzed across customers large enough to make avoided waste economically meaningful.

### Acquisition motion

Use founder-led content rather than broad paid acquisition.

LinkedIn content pillars:
1. **CPL is lying** — examples where low CPL creates poor CAC.
2. **What changed?** — anonymized diagnostic breakdowns.
3. **Creative economics** — hook/angle examples linked to qualification and sales.
4. **Build in public** — product decisions, screenshots and experiment learnings.

Primary CTA:

> “I’m opening a small number of AdSignal beta workspaces for teams that run paid media and track lead outcomes. Apply for a performance leak audit.”

Use an application form instead of high-volume cold outreach. Ask for monthly spend range, platform, CRM, lead volume and primary acquisition problem.

## Phase 2 — Monetization

### Beta offer
- free or highly discounted first diagnostic in exchange for structured feedback;
- paid recurring workspace after the first useful finding;
- no custom consulting bundled into the base SaaS.

### Commercial packaging
- Solo: independent media buyers;
- Pro: companies / multi-account operators;
- Agency: multiple client workspaces, white-label reporting and portfolio health.

The strongest pricing anchor is not number of charts. It is **media spend under decision support + number of workspaces**.

## Phase 3 — Distribution loops

### Agency partner loop
Agencies can use AdSignal as their client-facing proof layer. A branded executive report exposes the product to every client and creates a natural multi-workspace expansion path.

### Startup partnership loop
Offer an early-stage startup plan only to companies that already have meaningful paid acquisition and CRM data. “Startup” alone is not an ICP; measurable paid-acquisition pain is.

### Community / launch loop
After retention proof:
- LinkedIn launch;
- Product Hunt / international maker communities for English-first acquisition;
- Brazilian SaaS / growth communities;
- partner content with performance marketers and fractional CMOs.

## Fundraising strategy

### Do not raise merely because the product exists
Private capital becomes strategically useful after evidence that the recurring workflow is valued.

### Angel / accelerator readiness gate
Prepare a pitch when we can show:
- working product;
- repeated usage;
- first revenue;
- clear ICP;
- conversion from beta to paid;
- customer evidence that decisions improve downstream economics;
- credible path from CSV validation to direct connectors.

### Current ecosystem fit (September 2026)

**ACE Ventures** — strong thesis fit once initial traction exists: B2B/B2B2C, SaaS preference, validation/seed and stated investments up to R$3M.

**Bossa Invest** — relevant seed-stage route for a B2B SaaS once operating and showing traction.

**WOW Aceleradora** — relevant earlier-stage route; Batch #36 pre-registration is currently advertised, with initial investment up to R$500K and follow-on possibility.

**Antler Brazil** — potentially relevant for a founder choosing a full-time, venture-scale path; current published package can total up to R$2M including matched co-investment. The residency expects significant founder commitment in São Paulo.

**Anjos do Brasil** — appropriate channel for angel evaluation and investor introductions once pitch materials and early evidence are ready.

### Too early today

**Cubo Itaú** is not a near-term target: its current startup criteria emphasize validated traction and annual revenue above R$1M, and membership is paid.

**Google for Startups Accelerator Brazil** is a later milestone: the current program targets revenue-generating Seed–Series A companies and expects a larger technical team.

## Fundraising story

The pitch should not be “we use AI for ads.”

It should be:

1. Brazil alone invested tens of billions of reais in digital advertising annually.
2. Ad platforms optimize platform-visible conversions, while commercial value often lives downstream in CRM and revenue data.
3. Teams still reconcile those layers manually.
4. AdSignal builds a persistent decision memory across spend, lead quality, creative concepts and outcomes.
5. The moat compounds through account-specific experiment history and workflow integration, not through generic LLM access.

## Metrics investors should eventually see

- MRR / ARR;
- workspace retention;
- number of connected or imported ad accounts;
- media spend analyzed;
- weekly/monthly active workspaces;
- time to first actionable finding;
- percentage of recommendations acted on;
- experiment win/loss learning volume;
- expansion from one workspace to agency portfolio;
- gross margin after connector and AI costs.

## Rule

**Customers first, capital second.** The next dollar of development should make AdSignal harder to replace, not merely more impressive in a pitch deck.
