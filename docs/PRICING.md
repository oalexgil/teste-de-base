# AdSignal Intelligence — Commercial Model

## Positioning

AdSignal is not another ad dashboard. It is a decision layer that connects paid-media efficiency, lead quality and creative performance.

## Initial plans

### Free
- 1 workspace
- 1 report per month
- CSV imports
- overall AdSignal score
- limited recommendations

### Solo — target US$19–29/month
- recurring CSV analysis
- full recommendations
- historical comparisons
- executive reports
- one user

### Pro — target US$49–79/month
- multiple workspaces
- deeper history and benchmarks
- exports
- scheduled analysis when integrations arrive
- priority integrations

### Agency — target US$149–249/month
- multi-client workspaces
- team seats
- white-label reporting
- portfolio view
- client-level health alerts

## Expansion revenue

Potential add-ons after validation:
- additional data connectors;
- additional workspaces;
- AI creative classification credits;
- white-label exports;
- API access;
- historical data retention.

## Cost-control principles

The MVP deliberately avoids mandatory LLM/API calls. Core metrics and recommendations are deterministic, which keeps gross margins high and makes the product usable even before paid integrations are added.

When AI is introduced, it should be used for high-value tasks such as creative taxonomy extraction from ad copy/images, natural-language summaries, anomaly explanation and experiment generation. Usage should be metered or plan-limited.
