# Competitive Edge Hypothesis

AdSignal should not compete by having more charts. Its defensibility should come from joining datasets that teams routinely evaluate separately.

## Core wedge

### 1. Cost-to-quality bridge
Most ad interfaces optimize visible conversion volume. AdSignal emphasizes CPL → CPQL → meeting cost → CAC.

### 2. Creative-to-revenue bridge
Creative reporting should not stop at CTR. Concepts should eventually be compared against qualified-lead and sale economics where attribution permits.

### 3. Root-cause recommendation
The product should distinguish likely pre-click, click-to-page, page-to-lead, lead-quality and sales-handoff problems before suggesting the next action.

### 4. Explainability
Every recommendation exposes its evidence and limitations. This is especially important for agencies that need to justify decisions to clients.

## Moat path

The strongest future moat is not an LLM wrapper. It is the accumulated normalized history linking spend, downstream lead states, creative taxonomy and outcomes across repeated decision cycles.
