# AdSignal Validation Plan

## What must be proven before building expensive integrations

1. Users can understand the three scores without explanation.
2. CPQL and CAC change decisions compared with CPL-only analysis.
3. Creative concept grouping helps users decide what to produce next.
4. Recommendations are trusted because they expose evidence.
5. Users are willing to return with updated exports.
6. At least one customer segment is willing to pay for history, multi-workspace reporting or connectors.

## First beta cohort

Recommended target:
- freelancers managing multiple ad accounts;
- small agencies with 3–20 clients;
- lead-generation businesses spending enough for waste to be noticeable;
- ecommerce teams without enterprise attribution tooling.

## Interview / test tasks

Give users a prepared workspace and ask them to answer:
- Which campaign is actually healthiest?
- Where is the biggest acquisition leak?
- Is the lowest CPL campaign also the best business outcome?
- Which creative concept should receive the next test budget?
- What is the first action you would take tomorrow?

Record whether they can answer using AdSignal faster than their existing workflow.

## Early willingness-to-pay test

Do not ask only “would you pay?”. Offer concrete beta tiers and measure whether users choose to start a trial or pre-commit to a paid plan.

Primary hypothesis:
> A user who can identify avoidable paid-media waste or bad lead quality from one report will value recurring diagnostics more than a generic dashboard.
